DisplayXR Creator Hub

Make 3D, push it to HQ

Three pro-style creator tools that run in glasses-free 3D in the DisplayXR Browser on a Leia display, and in 2D in any browser. Upload your own files or build from scratch, then push the result to the DisplayXR HQ Creator Gallery.

MF
Materials & textures

Material Forge

Substance-style layered PBR materials.

  • 14 tileable generators, masks, blend modes
  • Sample colours and whole materials from photos
  • Paint straight onto 3D objects
  • Build objects from primitives or upload GLB/OBJ/STL
VS
Automotive visualization

Vehicle Studio

VRED-style car configurator and presenter.

  • Coupe, sedan, SUV, or upload your own car
  • Paint finishes, rims, calipers, glass, interior
  • Variant sets, viewpoints, turntable, renders
  • Studio, showroom, sunset, night and HDR lighting
TS
Geospatial

Terra Studio

ArcGIS-style 3D scenes from real terrain.

  • Real elevation with imagery, topo or street basemaps
  • Contours, elevation tint, water level, OSM buildings
  • GeoJSON, CSV and heightmap upload
  • Identify, measure, profile, line of sight, sketch
