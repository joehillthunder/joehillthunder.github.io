Leia Inc. · switchable glasses-free 3D

The phone that switches to 3D

A normal 2D phone that switches to glasses-free 3D in milliseconds, using content people already have.

Open an app on the phone. Photos turns one flat picture into depth with AI. Game puts a race track between your hand and the screen. 3D Call puts the caller in front of their room. Learn floats a planet in front of the glass. Switch to 2D at any time: the screen stays sharp.

You're seeing the 2D preview. Open this page in the DisplayXR Browser on a Leia display, and the phone screen weaves into real depth.

  • Tap on the phone screen to use the app.
  • Drag to turn the screen and look around the depth.
  • Use Switch to 2D to see the same screen flat.

Why it matters

One screen, a visible reason to choose a phone

For people

Photos, games, video and calls with depth, and no glasses. Every picture already in the gallery can become 3D.

For phone makers

A difference a shopper sees in five seconds in the store. It's a reason to choose the top model in a line, with apps and services that keep earning after the sale.

For operators

3D video, calls and games are a reason to move people to 5G plans, with content bundles and in-store demos that are easy to share.

What's inside

Three layers, added like touch

Switchable display

A thin optical layer on the panel switches between sharp 2D and glasses-free 3D in milliseconds.

Spatial AI

The front camera follows the viewer's eyes, and predictive tracking keeps 3D clean as the hand and head move.

AI 2D-to-3D

Photos, video and apps people already have are converted into depth in real time, so content is ready on day one.
