Step into the call, in 3D

Splat Call turns an ordinary 2D webcam into a live 3D Gaussian-splat hologram, frame by frame, right in the browser. Nothing is uploaded: the models run on this PC's GPU.

1 · TrackFace landmarks and a person mask (MediaPipe)
2 · DepthDepth Anything V2 or 3 on the head crop (WebGPU)
3 · ScaleIris size and landmarks fix depth in metres
4 · SplatA 3D Gaussian per pixel, sorted and woven

Inspired by VoluMe (Microsoft Research, 2025), which trains a network to predict these per-pixel Gaussians. Its weights aren't public, so this demo builds them from open models instead. Works in any WebGPU browser; in the DisplayXR Browser on a Leia display it's woven into glasses-free 3D.

Only the eye-tracking camera was found

This PC's only camera is the Leia display's eye tracker (“SR Camera”). Using it here can interrupt the eye tracking that the 3D effect depends on.

For the best result, plug in a USB webcam. You can also open a video, play the sample clip, or join a call and see the other person in 3D.

Cameras

Any webcam works. Splat Call recognises over 60 models by name or USB id, including Logitech, Razer, Elgato, Microsoft, Insta360 and OBSBOT webcams, phones used as webcams, HDMI capture cards, RealSense, Kinect and Orbbec depth cameras, and ZED and other side-by-side stereo cameras (it uses the left eye). Plug one in at any time and it's offered straight away.

    3D snapshot

    Freeze one frame and rebuild it with a model too big to run live. The snapshot replaces the live view until you press Live. Both run through a small helper on this PC.

    Start it from the app folder: pip install -r helper/requirements.txt then python helper/splat_helper.py. Add --mock-sharp --mock-immersity to try the flow without the model or keys.

    Apple SHARP

    One photo to about a million metric 3D Gaussians in one pass, around a second on a good GPU. It rebuilds the whole scene, not just the head. Runs on this PC's GPU through DirectML (1.3 GB model). Apple research licence: non-commercial.

    Immersity AI

    Leia's cloud 2D-to-3D depth for the head crop, turned into splats by this app. Needs an Immersity API client id and secret; each snapshot costs credits.

    About Splat Call 3D

    Every frame from the camera becomes a new cloud of up to 131,000 3D Gaussians. Nothing is pre-scanned and there's no avatar: what you see is the live pixels, lifted into 3D.

    • Track: MediaPipe finds 478 face landmarks and segments the person from the background.
    • Depth: Depth Anything (V2 Small by default, or 3 Small, or V2 Base) predicts relative depth for a square crop around the head and shoulders.
    • Snapshot: freeze one frame through a bigger model: Apple SHARP (about a million real 3D Gaussians of the whole scene) or Immersity AI's cloud depth, through a small helper on this PC.
    • Scale: the iris (about 11.7 mm across) gives the camera distance, and a least-squares fit to the landmark depths turns the network's output into metres, every frame.
    • Splat: each pixel becomes a flat Gaussian spanning its cell, oriented by the surface. A mirrored back layer gives the head volume, and a temporal filter keeps still regions steady.
