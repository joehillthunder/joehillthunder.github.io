Why it works in 3D

The camera stays side-on at a near-constant distance, so both fighters stay near the screen plane, where stereo is sharpest. The arena sits well behind them, which gives strong depth without your eyes ever having to refocus far.

Sparks, Heat bursts and Rage Arts are the showpieces: particle effects that come out past the glass toward you. The health bars and timer sit on top, and are most comfortable right at the screen plane.

Try the scene above: tap Red or press Throw a jab. Chain hits inside a second for a combo, and the bigger sparks fly further out of the screen.

Play the real game in 3D

  1. You need an Acer SpatialLabs laptop or a SpatialLabs View display. TrueGame doesn't run on other screens.
  2. Install Tekken 8 on Steam, then open the SpatialLabs TrueGame app and find Tekken 8 in its list.
  3. Pick the 3D Ultra profile and launch the game from TrueGame so the profile is applied.
  4. Sit square to the screen so eye tracking keeps you in the sweet spot. Fighting games are fast, so leave frame-rate headroom: stereo renders every frame twice.

Tekken 7 has had a 3D Ultra profile since 2023, if you want to try the effect on the older game first.
