    Where to play

    Platforms

    Both big platforms use eye tracking to aim a left and right image at each eye, so there are no glasses, but each needs its own hardware and only runs games that have a profile.

    Acer SpatialLabs TrueGame 218 games

    Runs on Acer SpatialLabs laptops and the SpatialLabs View displays. Each game has a pre-tuned profile, in one or both of two modes:

    • 3D Ultra: true stereo rendering with eye tracking. The best effect, and what all the newest profiles use.
    • 3D+: the older mode, for games without an Ultra profile.

    Newest profiles: Tekken 8, Elden Ring, Diablo III, Resident Evil 4 and Village, Black Myth: Wukong, Wo Long, WUCHANG, Little Nightmares III, A Plague Tale: Requiem and The Finals.

    DIY on PC Legacy

    The community tools from the Nvidia 3D Vision era still exist. 3D Fix Manager installs Helix and similar fixes, but it targets 3D Vision hardware and old drivers, and Nvidia no longer supports its stereo drivers.

    It's fun for retro DirectX 9 and 11 games, but for anything modern an official profile is the way to go.

    What to look for

    What makes a game great in 3D

    Rules of thumb from hands-on reviews of TrueGame and Odyssey 3D, and from how stereo rendering works.

    • Steady camera distanceFighting games, isometric RPGs and third-person games keep the action at one depth. Games where the camera whips around pull the depth around too.
    • Near and far layersForeground props, a mid-ground character and a far horizon give your eyes the most to work with. Flat, open fields read as flat.
    • A HUD that sits at the glassHealth bars and text are most comfortable on the screen plane. A HUD drawn at the wrong depth is distracting and tiring.
    • Stay in the sweet spotEye tracking widens it, but the effect still breaks down if you lean far off-centre. Sit square to the screen at normal monitor distance.
    • Frame rate firstStereo renders the scene twice. Leave headroom: drop a preset rather than let fast games stutter.
    • Turn depth down, not offModerate depth is more comfortable over long sessions than maximum pop-out. Save the big pop for cutscenes and showpieces.
