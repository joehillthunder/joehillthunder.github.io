The world: SimReady assets

Everything here comes from Lightwheel's kitchen_with_orange scene and SO-101 arm, the assets their open-source LeIsaac uses in Isaac Lab, converted from USD and running in your browser. Tap an asset in the scene or below.

Domain randomisation

The task trains on randomised copies of this kitchen so a policy can't memorise one layout. These ranges are LeIsaac's own (pick_orange_env_cfg.py).

Demonstrations

LightwheelAI/leisaac-pick-orange: 60 teleoperated episodes, 30 fps, recorded with a leader arm in Isaac Lab. The arm here replays each one's recorded joints; the cameras are Lightwheel's own renders, streamed from Hugging Face.

Make more data

Synthetic demos come from a port of Lightwheel's datagen state machine (datagen/state_machine/pick_orange.py) running in randomised layouts. Teleop: WASD move, R/F up/down, Space gripper, Q/E roll, Enter save.

Train a policy

Behaviour cloning: a small neural network learns to copy the demonstrations, mapping what the robot senses (joints, gripper, where the target is) to how it moves next. It trains here, in a web worker.

What the network sees and does

Input (15 numbers): the six joint positions, the tool point in the robot's base frame, the vector from the tool to its current goal (the next orange, or the plate while holding one, or home), and three flags: holding, oranges placed, done. Output: the five arm joints' motion over the next 0.2 s and the gripper command, so the policy runs at 30 Hz like the dataset.

Behaviour cloning on its own drifts: small errors take the robot to states no demo covers. DAgger (Ross et al. 2011) runs the policy, asks the expert what it should have done in every state it reached, adds those labels and retrains. The expert is the synthetic-data state machine, which can answer for any state.

Evaluate

Benchmarking in the spirit of Lightwheel's RoboFinals: many randomised episodes in parallel environments, scored with LeIsaac's success test (all three oranges on the plate and the arm back at rest) and its subtask terms.

Train a robot, end to end

A complete physical-AI loop in one page, on Lightwheel's simulation assets and data: a SimReady kitchen, real teleoperated demonstrations, synthetic data from a scripted expert, a policy trained in your browser, and a randomised benchmark that scores it.

  1. World SimReady assets and domain randomisation
  2. Demonstrations 60 Lightwheel teleop episodes, plus synthetic and your own
  3. Train behaviour cloning, then DAgger
  4. Evaluate parallel randomised episodes and a leaderboard

On a glasses-free 3D display in the DisplayXR Browser the kitchen and the arm are in real depth. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
