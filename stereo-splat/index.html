How it works: the camera module of the DisplayXR web SDK finds the 3D camera. It accepts the DisplayXR Browser's rectified "3D Camera" or any device that sends side-by-side frames wider than 2.5:1, and saves the raw pair. Depth comes from 7×7 census matching with semi-global aggregation and a left/right check. Each pixel becomes one Gaussian, placed in 3D through the pinhole Z = f·B/d. splat-transform writes the .sog. The page adds a DisplayXR camera block to its meta.json, so the viewer reopens the splat from the camera that took it. The preview is mirrored like a selfie. The splat is not mirrored: it is what the camera saw.
